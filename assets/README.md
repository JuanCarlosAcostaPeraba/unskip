# Application artwork

The Unskip mark combines a direct message path, a letter `U`, and a northeast delivery arrow. Its white and mint strokes use the application's dark-teal surface, remain distinct in monochrome, and avoid Microsoft, employer, cloud, and notification-sound imagery.

- `unskip-logo.svg` is the editable master.
- `unskip-logo.png` is the 1,024 pixel transparent-ready marketing export.
- `src/Unskip.App/Assets/unskip.ico` contains the Windows application sizes from 16 through 256 pixels.

The logo was developed from an image-generation concept and redrawn as deterministic vector geometry for crisp small-size rendering. Keep the proportions and colors aligned across future exports. Do not substitute third-party or employer artwork.
